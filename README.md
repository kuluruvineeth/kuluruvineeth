### What I teach

I record real builds — including the parts that break — and cut them into something watchable.

- **[Go microservices, 38 parts](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLJMBE6lUQTBQ)** — auth, product and search services over gRPC, with Postgres, MongoDB, Kafka, RabbitMQ and Elasticsearch. Built in order, mistakes included.
- **[System design walkthroughs](https://www.youtube.com/@kuluruvineeth)** — WhatsApp, Tinder, Yelp, Dropbox and others, drawn out rather than recited.
- **Distributed-systems patterns** — scaling reads and writes, contention, long-running jobs, large blobs. One failure per video, animated.

The teaching and the building are the same work. I explain systems well because I keep
breaking them first.
